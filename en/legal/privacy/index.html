Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Aleph Studio collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use our products and website.

1. Collection of Information

We collect information you provide directly to us, including username, email address, and Discord account details if you link it with our site.

2. How We Use Data

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide and maintain our services and products;
  • Authenticate your account and activate licenses;
  • Communicate with you regarding updates or important announcements;
  • Display your profile on the support page if you made a donation.

3. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.
